## Frequently asked questions

### How do I add my child's device?

1. On your phone, open the child's page and go to **Security**.
2. Tap **Pair a device** to show a pairing code and a QR code.
3. On the child's device, install etruvio, choose **Child Device Setup**, and scan the QR code or type the code.

The code is single-use and expires after 15 minutes. You can pair several devices for the same child.

### The child says a mission is done. Where do I approve it?

Missions that need your approval appear in the **Needs you** card on the child's page and in the **inbox** (bell icon). Tap **Approve** to award the stars, or **Reject** if the mission should be redone.

### My child bought a privilege by mistake. Can I refund it?

Yes. Open the child's page, go to the **wallet**, and pick the privilege:

- **Not now** puts it back in the child's wallet to use later.
- **Decline and refund** cancels it and returns the stars to the child's balance.

### Screen time is not being enforced on the child's iPhone

Screen time on iOS is enforced through Apple's Screen Time. On the child's device, etruvio must be allowed under **Settings → Screen Time**. If the permission was removed, open etruvio on the child's device and grant it again.

### How do I remove a device or a child?

- A device: child's page → **Security** → the device → **Remove**.
- A child profile: **Settings → Members** → the child → **Delete**.

### How do I delete my family or my account?

- The whole family and all its data: **Settings → Families → Delete family**. This is immediate and cannot be undone.
- Your account itself (email and login): **Settings → Security → Delete my account**. Families you own are deleted with it. You can also email **privacy@etruvio.ro** and we delete it within 30 days.

### I did not receive the invitation email

Check the spam folder for a message from etruvio. The invitation link expires after 7 days; the parent who invited you can resend it from **Settings → Members**.
